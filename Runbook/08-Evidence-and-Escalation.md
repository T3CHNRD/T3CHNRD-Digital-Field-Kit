# Evidence and Escalation SOP

Use this procedure whenever a case may continue beyond the current technician session.

## Evidence minimum

- case date and time
- technician and device identity
- user, location, and business impact
- issue description and initial state
- tool names, arguments, exit codes, and report paths
- screenshots or logs required to explain the finding
- actions taken and the approval for impactful changes
- validation result and unresolved symptoms

## Evidence handling

- Preserve original reports before editing or summarizing them.
- Use the Diagnostic-Reports directory or approved installed report location.
- Avoid placing credentials, secrets, or unrelated personal data in notes.
- Do not upload logs or suspicious files to external services without authorization.
- Keep timestamps and filenames intact when transferring evidence.

## Escalation packet

Provide a short summary, impact, timeline, device facts, reproduction steps, diagnostics already run, key evidence paths, changes made, and the specific decision needed from the next team.

## Closure

A case is ready to close when the user-impacting symptom is resolved or an owner has accepted the documented next action. Record the remaining risk and follow-up date when the issue is unresolved.
